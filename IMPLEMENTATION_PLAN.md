# BLACKMAIL.OM implementation plan

## Phase 1 — Foundation (current)

- Laravel 12 application, stable dependencies, environment baseline.
- Arabic-first locale, RTL/LTR layout, accessible design tokens and dark mode.
- Filament administration panel with least-privilege access foundation.
- Database schema, Eloquent models, factories, and safe demonstration seed data.
- Production-quality homepage and first public routes.
- Security headers, rate limiting, privacy defaults, and foundation tests.

## Phase 2 — Knowledge platform

- Complete article/category/tag resources, editorial approval workflow, safe rich text.
- Encyclopedia search, country directory, legal references, and FAQs.

## Phase 3 — Interactive services

- Anonymous no-storage help wizard, digital protection tools, consultation workflow.
- Encrypted case fields, retention automation, strict support authorization, audit events.

## Phase 4 — Production preparation

- Threat review, accessibility and performance audits, expanded automated tests.
- Sitemap/structured data, queue/scheduler configuration, deployment and backup drills.
